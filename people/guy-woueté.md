bio: >-
  **Guy Woueté** (b. 1980, Cameroon; lives and works in Antwerp, Brussels,
  Douala, and Penja) is a multidisciplinary artist, researcher, and educator at
  ERG (École de Recherche Graphique, Brussels). His practice investigates
  artistic creation as an epistemic site and an instituting process, examining
  the structural residue of coloniality, capitalist extraction, and migration
  regimes. Moving across installation, video, performance, sculpture, painting,
  and publishing, his work develops critical dispositifs grounded in collective
  study and local realities. A graduate of Université Paris 8 and ERG, and a
  former resident at the Rijksakademie van beeldende kunsten in Amsterdam
  (2009–2010), Woueté has exhibited internationally, including at the 60th
  Venice Biennale (2024), Gwangju Biennale (2024), Palais de Tokyo (2025), and
  Lubumbashi Biennale (2019). His work is held in major collections such as M
  HKA, S.M.A.K., KADIST, FRAC des Pays de la Loire, and the World Bank Art
  Collection.
