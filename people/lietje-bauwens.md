bio: >-
  Lietje Bauwens is a filmmaker, researcher, writer and organizer who works with
  film as a tool for territorial resistance, studying how strategies of struggle
  are represented on screen. In her films *WTC A Love Story* (2020) and *WTC A
  Never-Ending Love Story* (2023), made with Wouter De Raeve, she uses film to
  intervene in debates surrounding the redevelopment of Brussels’ Northern
  Quarter, investigating both the history and the present state of resistance in
  the area. With Caillou Filmfest, which she organizes with Lea Vromman, she
  brings films in this tradition into dialogue with people engaged in, or
  affected by, territorial struggles. Across projects and publications such
  as *Perhaps It Is High Time for a Xeno-Architecture to Match* (Sternberg
  Press, 2020) and *Speculative Facts* (Onomatopee, 2022), she has investigated
  fiction and speculation as research methodologies within spatial and political
  debates. She has also written extensively on these themes in essays for
  magazines including *Etcetera*, *Rekto Verso*, *De Witte Raaf*, *mister
  Motley* and *nY*, the literary magazine she is a proud editor of. She finished
  a Masters in Philosophy at the University of Amsterdam in 2015, was a resident
  at the Jan van Eyck academy from 2018-2019, and started her PhD in the Arts at
  Sint Lucas Antwerpen (KdG) and ARIA University of Antwerp) in 2026.
