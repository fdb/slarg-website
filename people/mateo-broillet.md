bio: >-
  Mateo Broillet (1990, CH) is a graphic designer and teacher living and working
  in Belgium. After his studies in Lausanne and Amsterdam, he established his
  practice under the moniker Atelier Mateo Broillet (AMB), collaborating with
  design studios and a variety of clients. He has been teaching graphic design
  since 2021 in the visual and graphic communication department at ENSAV La
  Cambre and since 2022 at Sint Lucas Antwerpen. He recently initiated Ambtype,
  a digital type foundry specialising in typographic research and revivals.
