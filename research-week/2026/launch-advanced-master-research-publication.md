We are launching the Advanced Master of Research in Art and Design's yearly research publication!

"What Your Research Did To Me," features contributions from the artist-researchers Jelle Michiels, Déborah Claire, Joud Toamah, Manju Sharma, L. Puska, Vivi Touloumidi, Yasmine Akondo, and Parvin Saljoughi.

From a "menu" of prompts, each artist chose one written by a peer and answered it with their contribution to the publication. In this way, the different contributions become entangled with each other, mimicking a process that often occurs during the research year at AdMa. In a research environment that brings together artists with very different research subjects and approaches, the intense exchange often ends up influencing them in unexpected ways.
